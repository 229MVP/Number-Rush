# Demo Video Script

**Target length:** 45–75 seconds
**Format:** Screen recording, portrait, 390×844 or a real device.

## Sequence

1. **(0:00–0:04) Splash animation** — let the logo pulse briefly, then tap "TAP TO START."
   *Caption: "Number Rush — Neon Number Puzzle"*
2. **(0:04–0:08) Main Menu** — brief hold on PLAY / HOW TO PLAY / STATS / SETTINGS.
   *Caption: "Clean, responsive Main Menu"*
3. **(0:08–0:11) Start game** — tap PLAY, show the fresh Classic board.
   *Caption: "Place tiles. Hit exactly 21."*
4. **(0:11–0:20) Place tiles** — a few normal placements across different lanes.
5. **(0:20–0:26) Trigger Perfect** — land exactly on 21; show the lane's "PERFECT!" state and score bump.
   *Caption: "Perfect clear = bonus points"*
6. **(0:26–0:34) Trigger combo** — chain a second consecutive Perfect; show the combo multiplier changing (×1 → ×2).
   *Caption: "Chain Perfects for a bigger combo"*
7. **(0:34–0:40) Demonstrate Bust** — overshoot 21 on a lane; show the strike counter drop.
   *Caption: "Overshoot 21 and you lose a strike"*
8. **(0:40–0:47) Demonstrate Multiplier** — select Multiplier, place a doubled tile.
   *Caption: "Multiplier doubles your next tile"*
9. **(0:47–0:54) Demonstrate Swap** — select Swap, exchange two lane totals.
   *Caption: "Swap lane totals to recover a bad board"*
10. **(0:54–1:02) Reach Game Over** — lose the third strike; show the Game Over summary (score, best score, stats).
11. **(1:02–1:07) Play Again** — tap PLAY AGAIN, show a fresh board start instantly.
    *Caption: "Instant replay — no loading screens"*
12. **(1:07–1:12) Final logo card** — cut to the "NUMBER RUSH" wordmark + product title text.
    *Caption: "Number Rush — Neon Number Puzzle Game Template. Available now."*

## Notes

- Do not claim features that aren't shipped in this SKU (no leaderboard,
  accounts, or purchases footage).
- Keep captions on-screen only as long as the action they describe.
- Record from the actual running app (Expo Go, a dev/preview build, or the
  web export) — do not use mockup stills.
