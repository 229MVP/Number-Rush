# Support Policy — Number Rush Template

## Support period

**30 days** from the date of purchase, unless your marketplace's own policy
(e.g. Codester, CodeCanyon) states a different window — the marketplace's
policy takes precedence where applicable.

## Included in support

- Clarifying installation/setup steps in `DOCUMENTATION.md`
- Bug reports for issues in the **included source code**, reproduced against
  an unmodified copy of the template
- Confirming a documented file/asset is missing from your download
- Basic configuration questions (e.g. "where do I change the target value?")

## Not included in support

- Custom feature development (new game modes, new screens, new mechanics)
- App Store / Google Play account setup, submission, or review handling
- Building or configuring a backend (Supabase or otherwise)
- Ad network (AdMob) or payment processor (RevenueCat/App Store/Play Billing)
  integration and account setup
- Major redesigns, rebranding, or reskinning work (available as a separate
  paid service — see `marketplace/DIRECT_SALES_PAGE.md`)
- Compatibility fixes required after you upgrade third-party dependencies
  beyond the versions shipped in this template
- Support for heavily modified copies of the source where the reported issue
  cannot be reproduced against the original template

## How to request support

Follow the process specified by the marketplace you purchased from (e.g. the
Codester/CodeCanyon comment or support-ticket system for that item). Include:

- Expo SDK / package versions (`npx expo-doctor` output is helpful)
- Platform (iOS/Android/Web) and device or simulator details
- Exact steps to reproduce, ideally against an unmodified copy of the template

We do not promise unlimited free customization work under this policy.
