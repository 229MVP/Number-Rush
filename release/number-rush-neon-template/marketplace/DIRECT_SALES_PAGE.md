## What you get

- Complete, tested Classic Rush 21 gameplay loop
- First-time tutorial, Multiplier + Swap power-ups
- Local best-score tracking and Stats screen
- Settings (audio/haptics/accessibility/reset)
- Full TypeScript source you can extend or reskin
- Documentation: setup, customization guide, game rules, asset licenses,
  known limitations
- Responsive layout tested across common phone sizes + Expo Web

## What this is not

- Not a finished, store-published app — you configure your own bundle
  identifiers, icons, and store listings.
- Not a backend product — no accounts, cloud save, live leaderboard, ads, or
  purchases are included or enabled.
- Not unlimited custom development — see the Support Policy for what's
  included in the purchase price.
